Budget & Resources

The US$1.5M budget by category and period, the team's level of effort and the cost questions to settle early.

For: Leadership & funderStatus: Draft v0.1Updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • Total request US$1,500,000: US$1,335,968 direct and US$164,032 indirect (12% rate stated).
  • Subawards to partners are the largest line (US$800,000 cumulative), then WSU personnel and benefits (US$308,941) and consultants (US$157,838).
  • Technology spend is small: one enterprise server (US$36,095), hosting (US$4,000 in each of Periods 1 and 3) and Starlink connectivity.
  • Three things to settle early: how the line items reconcile to the total, whether the server includes a GPU, and what KNPHI will need to fund after 2029.

Why it matters

The budget determines what can be built in-house versus by partners, how much hardware the AI layer gets, and whether the system can be sustained after the grant — a Gate 3 condition (“documented KNPHI recurrent budget pathway”). Spotting reconciliation and scope gaps now is cheaper than at a progress report.

Reconciliation note

The stated lines add up to US$72,728 more than the US$1.5M total. The direct-cost total (US$1,335,968) leaves US$727,272 for subawards once the other direct lines are subtracted — and US$727,272 plus 10% is exactly US$800,000. The likeliest explanation is that the subaward figure is quoted including subrecipients' own indirect costs. Also, US$164,032 is 12.3% of the stated direct total rather than exactly 12%. Confirm both against the detailed budget narrative before the first financial report. Open

4Payments and gates

Payment 2 is released at Gate 1 (Progress Report 1) and Payment 3 at Gate 2 (Progress Report 2); no payment is attached to Gate 3. Proposal A held gate therefore delays cash for the following period — keep partner subaward schedules aligned with gate dates. See doc 13.

5Technology cost observations

  • GPU. On-premise LLM inference needs at least one data-centre GPU. If the US$36,095 server quote does not include one, either re-scope the server, budget a GPU from savings, or use a Government-authorised hosted model. Open
  • Single server. No line covers a second node or recovery site. Explore DHA/Government shared infrastructure for disaster recovery (doc 11).
  • Software licences. The recommended stack is open source, so there are no licence lines — the costs are people and hardware.
  • Penetration testing and load testing (Gate 2) should be explicitly costed, for example within the enterprise software consultant line.

• KNPHI owns RADAR and is its primary user; DHA sets standards and hosting rules; WSU holds the grant and leads integration.
  • Each partner owns a distinct workstream: UM/CGHE the AI-enabled DMT-PHE, CGP deployment, KEMRI evaluation, KIPRE One Health, an enterprise partner product hardening, Sobek the GHEC-AI interface.
  • A RACI table assigns who decides, who does the work, and who must be consulted for each major deliverable.
  • Three standing bodies keep decisions moving: steering committee, technical working group, data governance group.

RADAR crosses institutions that own different data, systems and mandates. Without clear ownership, decisions stall between partners — especially the MER standard, data access and model releases. Clear roles also make the KNPHI transition at Gate 3 a handover of something KNPHI already runs, not a surprise.

Reconciliation note

The stated lines add up to US$72,728 more than the US$1.5M total. The direct-cost total (US$1,335,968) leaves US$727,272 for subawards once the other direct lines are subtracted — and US$727,272 plus 10% is exactly US$800,000. The likeliest explanation is that the subaward figure is quoted including subrecipients' own indirect costs. Also, US$164,032 is 12.3% of the stated direct total rather than exactly 12%. Confirm both against the detailed budget narrative before the first financial report. Open

Technology cost observations

  • GPU. On-premise LLM inference needs at least one data-centre GPU. If the US$36,095 server quote does not include one, either re-scope the server, budget a GPU from savings, or use a Government-authorised hosted model. Open
  • Single server. No line covers a second node or recovery site. Explore DHA/Government shared infrastructure for disaster recovery (doc 11).
  • Software licences. The recommended stack is open source, so there are no licence lines — the costs are people and hardware.
  • Penetration testing and load testing (Gate 2) should be explicitly costed, for example within the enterprise software consultant line.

Doc 08·Operations

Signal-to-Response Workflow & 7-1-7

Who does what, in what order, from the first signal to an authorised response — including the 7-1-7 clock and the GHEC-AI hand-off.

For: EOC & surveillanceEngineeringM&E / researchStatus: Draft v0.1Updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • Nine steps take an event from first signal to authorised response and closure.
  • RADAR does the assembling, scoring and time-stamping; people make every decision.
  • Level 1 events can be authorised by the county; Level 2–3 go through KNPHI PHEOC before the GHEC-AI hand-off.
  • Each step writes a timestamp to the MER, so 7-1-7 performance is measured continuously instead of reconstructed after the fact.

Why it matters

This is the workflow EOC staff will be trained on and evaluated against. It is also where the time is lost today — in hand-overs between people and systems. Writing it down step by step makes clear who owns each hand-over, what RADAR automates, and what is measured at each point.

37-1-7 in practice

7-1-7 sets three timeliness targets: detect within 7 days of emergence, notify within 1 day, and complete early response actions within 7 days. Research The seven early response actions are:

  1. Initiate investigation or deploy an investigation/response team
  2. Conduct epidemiological analysis of burden, severity and risk factors, and an initial risk assessment
  3. Obtain laboratory confirmation of the outbreak aetiology
  4. Initiate appropriate case management and infection prevention and control measures in health facilities
  5. Initiate appropriate public-health countermeasures in affected communities
  6. Initiate appropriate risk communication and community engagement
  7. Establish a coordination mechanism

An action that does not apply is recorded as “N/A”, not left blank. For larger events, 7-1-7 is best applied in real time through an Early Action Review shortly after notification, feeding later intra- and after-action reviews. RADAR's event timeline is designed to be the working record for those reviews.

Why this changes things

Today 7-1-7 performance in Kenya is assessed retrospectively and case by case, because the timestamps that define it sit in different systems. Proposal With the MER, the same numbers are available live for every event — and the slowest step is visible while there is still time to act.

5The GHEC-AI hand-off

RADAR provides upstream intelligence, the DMT-PHE agent supports human-supervised classification and escalation, and GHEC-AI matches approved response needs to available workforce and capacity — RRT registry, training and skills matching, deployment readiness and mobilisation. Proposal The payload is defined in doc 05. Evaluating this hand-off (reliability, completeness and timeliness of information exchange, user experience, effect on mobilisation) is one of the four learning questions (doc 14).

6Worked example: suspected RVF in northern Kenya

Based on the scenario in the project deck; times are illustrative. Proposal

  1. Week 0 — rainfall anomaly. KMD data shows heavy rainfall; RADAR's RVF forecast prior rises for several northern sub-counties. No alert, but sensitivity increases.
  2. Week 4, Mon 09:00 — animal signal. KABS reports a cluster of livestock abortions. RADAR opens MER RVF-…-001 (detected_at); the score moves to “watch”.
  3. Mon 15:00 — human signal. m-Dharura reports two febrile patients with bleeding in the same sub-county. Linker attaches both to the MER; fusion moves the score to “alert”; signal card issued to the county.
  4. Tue 10:00 — verification. Sub-county officer confirms by phone and visit (verified_at).
  5. Tue 11:00 — classification. RVF trigger card rules return Level 2; agent drafts a recommendation (One Health investigation, joint human–animal response, samples to KEMRI/NPHL) with citations. County EOC manager confirms (classified_at, 26 h after detection).
  6. Tue 14:00 — national. KNPHI PHEOC confirms Level 2, activates support (escalated_at, notified_at).
  7. Tue 16:00 — hand-off. GHEC-AI proposes a joint human–animal RRT; EOC approves; teams mobilise (response_authorised_at).
  8. Days 2–7 — actions. Investigation, lab confirmation, IPC, community countermeasures, RCCE, vector control and coordination tracked to completion; 7-1-7 computed automatically.

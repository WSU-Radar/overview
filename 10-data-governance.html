Doc 10·Trust & Safety

Data Governance, Privacy & Ethics

The laws, approvals and agreements RADAR needs, how data is classified, where each class may live and how access is controlled.

For: Governance & legalEngineeringLeadership & funderStatus: Draft v0.1Updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • Health data is sensitive personal data under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019; the Digital Health Act 2023 and DHA set the sector rules.
  • Before live data flows: ethics approvals (KEMRI SERU, WSU IRB), data-sharing agreements for every MVP source, a DPIA lodged at least 60 days ahead, and registration checks. Ethics approvals and agreements are Gate 1 conditions; the DPIA and registration are legal prerequisites.
  • Data is handled in four classes; identifiable data lives only on Government-authorised infrastructure and never goes to public AI services.
  • Access is role-based, least-privilege, MFA-protected and fully logged.

Why it matters

No data, no RADAR — and data only flows when custodians trust the arrangements. Governance gaps are also the most likely cause of delay to the MVP: agreements with several institutions and a 60-day DPIA notice period sit on the critical path. Getting this right early protects people, the partners and the project timeline.

1Principles

Government custody

Restricted data stays on Government-controlled or authorised infrastructure. Proposal

Custodians decide

Each source institution remains the authority over access to its data.

Minimum necessary

Collect and share the least data, at the coarsest granularity, that serves the purpose.

Purpose-bound

Surveillance and response; research or secondary use needs separate approval.

2Legal and regulatory framework

Summary for orientation only — not legal advice. Confirm current requirements with KNPHI/DHA legal counsel and the ODPC. Research

InstrumentWhat it means for RADAR
Data Protection Act, 2019Health data is sensitive personal data. Lawful basis and purpose limitation; data-subject rights; DPIA where processing is likely to be high-risk; cross-border transfers only with safeguards; breach notification to the ODPC within 72 hours.
Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021DPIA report lodged with the Data Protection Commissioner at least 60 days before high-risk processing starts. Processing for the “strategic interest of the State” must use a server/data centre in Kenya (or keep a serving copy there) — confirm whether public-health surveillance is treated as such.
Digital Health Act, 2023Establishes the Digital Health Agency (DHA): the Comprehensive Integrated Health Information System, interoperability standards and certification of digital health solutions. Classifies health data; breach notification to DHA within 48 hours with follow-up detail within 72 hours; long retention duties for health records. The Act is operating while a court appeal on parts of it is pending.
Digital health regulations (2025)Including the Digital Health (Health Information Management Procedures) Regulations; check gazettement status and specific hosting and exchange obligations.
ODPC guidance on health dataPractical guidance on lawful processing of health data; use when drafting the DPIA and agreements.
International Health Regulations (2005), amended2024 amendments in force from 19 September 2025: a new “pandemic emergency” alert level and National IHR Authorities. Notification duties to WHO run through Kenya's IHR National Focal Point.
Research ethicsKEMRI Scientific and Ethics Review Unit (SERU) amendment and WSU IRB extension are Gate 1 conditions Proposal; informed consent or approved waiver for evaluation data collected from individuals.

6What every data-sharing agreement must cover

  • Purpose (surveillance and response; any research use listed separately)
  • Exact fields and granularity; frequency; access route
  • Roles (controller/processor), named stewards and contacts
  • Security controls and hosting location
  • Onward sharing (e.g. to GHEC-AI) — minimum-necessary fields only
  • Retention, return and deletion at the end of the grant; transition to KNPHI
  • Breach notification duties and timelines
  • Publication rules for aggregated or de-identified outputs

8Data lifecycle and open outputs

  • Curation: data dictionaries, metadata, harmonised coding, quality checks, de-identification where required, version-controlled documentation of derived variables. Proposal
  • Retention: align with Digital Health Act duties and KNPHI policy; raw landing data kept only as long as re-processing needs it.
  • Open outputs at Gate 3: de-identified or aggregated derivative datasets, metadata, analytic code, data dictionaries and model documentation, subject to Government of Kenya requirements. Proposal
  • Telecom, mobility and media data: only where legally and operationally permitted, minimum granularity, aggregated or de-identified. Proposal

9Open questions

  1. Is surveillance processing a “strategic interest of the State” under the 2021 Regulations, and what hosting obligations does the Digital Health Act add? Owner: KNPHI/DHA legal · month 1
  2. Who signs the DPIA and when is it lodged? Lodging by early January 2027 keeps a March 2027 data start possible. Owner: KNPHI + WSU · month 1
  3. One umbrella agreement or one per source? Owner: KNPHI data governance consultants
  4. Data residency for the GHEC-AI hand-off. Owner: KNPHI + Sobek

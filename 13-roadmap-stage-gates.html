Roadmap & Stage Gates

The three-year plan from MVP to KNPHI transition, the three funding gates, their milestones and the thresholds that must be met.

For: Leadership & funderEngineeringM&E / researchStatus: Draft v0.1Updated: 29 October 2026

In short

  • Three years, three stage gates: Gate 1 (MVP + national validation, to Sep 2027), Gate 2 (county deployment, to Sep 2028), Gate 3 (evaluation + transition, to Sep 2029, verified at the final report). Payments are attached to Gates 1 and 2.
  • The four-component MVP is a hard internal deadline: accepted by KNPHI by 30 June 2027. A partly delivered MVP cannot pass Gate 1.
  • Thresholds rise at each gate: concordance 0.85 → 0.88 → 0.90; SUS 75 → 78 → 80; documentation completeness 80% → 85% → 90%.
  • A few figures differ between the proposal, the stage-gate paper and the deck — they are listed here to settle at kick-off.

Why it matters

Payments are tied to gates, and each gate asks for evidence of both product quality (accuracy, usability) and real-world effect (7-1-7, latency). Planning backwards from those tests — especially the MVP deadline and the pre-deployment baseline, which cannot be recreated later — is the difference between passing on time and asking for a remediation window.

2Gate 1 — MVP delivered and platform validated

Reporting period November 2026 – September 2027. Decision: proceed to national and county deployment, or hold and remediate. Proposal

Milestones

  1. RADAR MVP demonstrated and accepted by KNPHI by June 2027, with all four components (MER; functioning data flows for priority sources; integrated DMT-PHE workflow; GHEC-AI interface specifications agreed with Sobek).
  2. Technical Product Profile and architecture signed off by WSU, UM/CGHE, KNPHI/DHA and the enterprise software partner.
  3. Dataset inventory and system mapping complete; priority connectors and API/MCP interfaces working; outbreak user guides drafted for all four categories; national DMT-PHE deployment plan approved.
  4. Eight-week parallel validation at the national EOC completed, generating a concordance dataset against independent expert-panel review.
  5. Governance conditions met: KEMRI SERU amendment, WSU IRB extension, data-sharing agreements/MOUs for all MVP data streams.
  6. Baseline measured and reported: detection-to-classification time, detection-to-escalation latency and 7-1-7 compliance at the national EOC before RADAR deployment.

Thresholds

  • AI–expert concordance ≥ 0.85 on the parallel-validation dataset
  • SUS ≥ 75 among national EOC users
  • DMT-PHE documentation completeness ≥ 80% across escalation events
  • Zero adverse decision events attributable to the AI layer
  • Minimum event record agreed with KNPHI and in operational use
  • Baseline 7-1-7 and detection-to-classification measures documented and agreed
MVP rule

If the MVP is not accepted by 30 June 2027, the consortium notifies the foundation within 30 days with a remediation plan — not waiting for the progress report. Gate 1 cannot be passed on a partially delivered MVP, because Gate 2 depends on all four components. Proposal

3Gate 2 — Deployed, hardened and validated in operational use

Reporting period October 2027 – September 2028. Decision: proceed to prospective evaluation and KNPHI transition, or hold scale-up. Proposal

Milestones

  1. RADAR and AI-enabled DMT-PHE in routine use at the national EOC and five priority county EOCs, with readiness assessments, ICT setup and training complete at every site.
  2. Product hardening complete: load, cybersecurity and penetration testing, interface validation, deployment on KNPHI/DHA-approved infrastructure, with test reports available to the foundation.
  3. GHEC-AI escalation-to-response hand-off validated in live workflow — only authorised, minimum-necessary information passes.
  4. EOC staff trained at national and county level with training records and competency results (Output 4).
  5. Validated outbreak user guides, algorithms, workflow and training materials for all four categories (Output 5).
  6. AI model retrained on national EOC operational data, with a documented benchmarking and recalibration procedure and a published error and override log.
  7. Minimum event record published and adopted as a KNPHI standard.

Thresholds

  • Dataset integration ≥ 80% of the baseline inventory
  • AI–expert concordance ≥ 0.88 at every deployment site
  • SUS ≥ 78 at every site
  • Documentation completeness ≥ 85%
  • Zero adverse decision events attributable to the AI layer
  • Median signal detection → structured DMT-PHE classification ≤ 48 hours
  • Measurable reduction vs. baseline in 7-1-7 detection and notification timelines at the national EOC and a majority of county sites

4Gate 3 — Evaluated, institutionalised and transitioned

Reporting period October 2028 – September 2029; verified at the final report; no payment attached. Decision: confirm close-out and inform any follow-on investment. Proposal

Milestones

  1. Prospective evaluation and endline assessment completed by WSU with KNPHI.
  2. KNPHI operational transition: system on Government infrastructure, admin rights transferred, maintenance, support and security procedures handed over.
  3. Recurrent budget pathway documented and reflected in a KNPHI budget submission or departmental statement.
  4. Scale-up evidence report and policy brief (Output 6).
  5. Two manuscripts submitted to peer-reviewed journals, with preprints.
  6. Regional dissemination through Africa CDC epidemic-intelligence networks and the WHO Preparedness Data Exchange.
  7. Open outputs released: de-identified/aggregated datasets, metadata, code, data dictionaries, model documentation.

Thresholds

  • AI–expert concordance ≥ 0.90 · SUS ≥ 80 · documentation completeness ≥ 90%
  • False-negative rate below the ceiling agreed at Gate 1
  • Sustained reduction in detection-to-escalation latency vs. baseline
  • Demonstrated improvement in 7-1-7 compliance vs. baseline at national and county sites

9Critical path

  1. Agreements and DPIA → no live data before they are in place.
  2. MER v0.1 → connectors, linker and DMT-PHE integration all depend on it.
  3. Baseline measurement → must happen before deployment changes behaviour; cannot be recreated later.
  4. Server/GPU procurement → staging and production depend on it; lead times can be months.
  5. MVP acceptance (30 June 2027) → leaves about three months for the eight-week parallel run and the Gate 1 report.

10Open questions

  1. Agree the reconciled numbers above and update all three source documents. Owner: PI + foundation · kick-off
  2. When exactly is the national parallel run? It must finish before the Gate 1 report. Owner: KNPHI + WSU
  3. What counts as “KNPHI acceptance” of the MVP? A signed acceptance protocol avoids ambiguity in June 2027. Owner: KNPHI + WSU
